Understanding the Water Crisis

Learn about the global water challenges and why conservation is critical

The Global Water Crisis

Water scarcity affects more than 40% of the global population, and this number is projected to rise due to climate change and population growth. The water crisis is not just about having enough water, but also about access to clean, safe water for drinking, sanitation, and hygiene.

Key Challenges

  • Population Growth: Increasing demand for water resources
  • Climate Change: Altered precipitation patterns and extreme weather
  • Pollution: Contamination of freshwater sources
  • Infrastructure: Aging water systems and lack of access
  • Agriculture: 70% of freshwater used for irrigation

The Impact of Water Scarcity

Health

Lack of clean water leads to waterborne diseases, affecting millions of people worldwide, particularly children under 5.

Education

Children, especially girls, miss school to collect water, perpetuating cycles of poverty and inequality.

Economy

Water scarcity costs the global economy billions of dollars annually through reduced productivity and healthcare costs.

Environment

Ecosystems suffer when water sources are depleted, affecting biodiversity and natural habitats.
