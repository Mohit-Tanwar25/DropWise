Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit a conservation project for your gallery?

Use the contact form above with the "Success Story" category. Include photos, water savings data, and project details. We love featuring inspiring conservation initiatives!

Can you help me calculate potential water savings for my home?

Contact us with details about your current usage and home setup. Our team can provide personalized recommendations and savings estimates.

Do you offer educational programs for schools?

Yes! We partner with schools to develop water conservation curricula. Contact us to discuss bringing our educational programs to your institution.

How can businesses partner with DropWise?

We welcome partnerships with businesses committed to water conservation. Use the "Partnership Inquiry" category to discuss collaboration opportunities.
